## Experiments and observations

| Test | Observed result | Evaluation |

| Second revision | Changed the claim to say the uploaded report did not establish whether water and waste received independent assurance. | More appropriate treatment of missing evidence. Specific assurance details still require source verification. |
| Mind map | Separated La Trobe commitments, UNSW outcomes and evidence limitations. | Helpful overview, but omitted responsible units and did not clearly explain the missing comparable performance evidence. |

## Overall assessment

The tool supported summarisation, source-based questions and study
material generation. Citations helped locate supporting passages,
but did not remove the need to inspect the original documents.

Human review identified overly broad interpretations and prompted
revisions. Source-based generation therefore should not be treated
as automatic factual verification.

The evaluation used only two institutional documents and a small
set of tasks. Institutional reporting may emphasise selected
achievements. These tests do not establish demographic bias,
general reliability or suitability for unsupervised academic use.
